## WHO indicators and other source material

WHO-reported indicators are reproduced in the WHO columns of the datasets,
`data/who_detail.csv`, and the WHO fields of `data/map-data.js` (`s`, `d`, `m`
and `e`). Spreadsheets may contain both source extracts and original analysis.
Retain the source attribution and consult the terms accompanying the original
dataset before redistributing source material. This project does not grant
additional rights in WHO material or imply WHO endorsement.

[WHO data terms](https://data.who.int/about/data/terms-and-conditions) describe
the general terms for data.who.int; the terms and metadata for the specific
source take precedence. Other linked registries, pharmacy websites and
publications likewise retain their own terms.

## Geography

The world topology uses [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas),
based on [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/).
Natural Earth map data are [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
