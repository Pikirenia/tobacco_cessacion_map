Smoking cessation medicines

The map shows how many of four first-line medicines people can actually obtain through legal retail in each of 195 countries: nicotine replacement therapy (NRT), varenicline, cytisine and bupropion. Hover over or tap a country to compare retail status with what the country reported to WHO.

Submit country feedback

    Select a legend item to highlight those countries. Small island states are shown as dots. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom.

    Retail status reflects practical consumer availability based on the latest information received: a medicine counts as available when at least one of two independent, consumer-facing retail sources shows a current way to buy, order or reserve it. Unavailable means both sources gave a confirmed negative result; uncertain means neither condition was met. Bupropion counts as available even where it is marketed only as an antidepressant.

    No data (white) marks countries where the status of all four medicines is uncertain; in most of them the medicines are probably not available through open retail channels. Grey areas are territories outside the 195-country study.

    WHO columns show what each country reported to WHO for 2024: whether the medicine is legally sold, its dispensing category (OTC or Rx) and whether its cost is reimbursed. WHO data describe legal status, not current stock, and may differ from retail findings.

    “Full set” means NRT, bupropion and at least one of varenicline or cytisine are all available.
